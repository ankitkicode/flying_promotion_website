items:
  - question: "How does a project usually start?"
    answer: "With a conversation about the problem, not a list of features. We ask what is happening today, what it costs you and what a good outcome would look like. If we are a fit, the next step is a written scope."
  - question: "How long does a project take?"
    answer: "It depends on scope. A focused website or automation can move quickly; a custom CRM or internal tool takes longer. We agree on stages and milestones before work begins rather than promising a single date up front."
  - question: "Do we own the website, code and accounts?"
    answer: "Yes. You own the domain, hosting, third-party accounts and the work we produce for you. We set things up in your name and hand over the access and documentation."
  - question: "Can you work with the tools we already use?"
    answer: "Usually, yes. We look at your current stack before recommending anything new. Replacing a tool is only worth it when it removes a real problem."
  - question: "How is pricing structured?"
    answer: "Most work is quoted as a fixed scope with clear deliverables. Ongoing support and improvements can be arranged separately. We explain what is included before you commit."
  - question: "What happens after launch?"
    answer: "You get a handover with documentation and access. From there, you can maintain the system yourself, work with your own developer, or ask us to stay involved for support and improvements."
