## Overview

This policy explains what information Flying Promotion collects through this website, why we collect it and how it is handled. It applies to this website and to enquiries made through it.

We keep the policy deliberately practical. If anything is unclear, you can ask us about it using the contact form.

## Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number and the details of your enquiry. If you contact us directly, we may also keep a record of that correspondence.

We may collect limited technical information automatically, such as the pages visited, the type of device used and general location derived from an IP address. This information is used to understand how the site is performing and to keep it secure.

## How we use information

We use the information you provide to:

- Respond to your enquiry and discuss a potential project
- Prepare a proposal or scope of work
- Provide services you have asked for
- Keep records of our communication and agreements
- Improve the website and our services
- Meet legal, accounting or regulatory obligations

We do not sell personal information. We do not use enquiry details to send unrelated marketing messages without your agreement.

## Cookies and analytics

This website may use essential cookies required for it to function. If analytics or similar tools are enabled, they may set cookies or use similar technologies to provide aggregated usage information.

Where required, non-essential cookies are used only with your consent. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

## Sharing information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate the website or deliver a project, such as hosting, email, analytics or payment providers. These providers receive only what is needed for their role and are expected to protect it.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a transfer of the business. We do not share enquiry details with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

## Retention

We keep enquiry and project information for as long as needed to respond, deliver the work and meet legal or accounting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it where practical.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Please avoid sending sensitive information, such as payment card details or government identification numbers, through the contact form.

## Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To make a request, contact us through the contact form. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

## Children

This website is intended for businesses and professional audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure the information receives an appropriate level of protection.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page. If a change is significant, we will make that clear on the website.

## Contact

If you have a question about this policy or how your information is handled, please use the contact form on this website.
